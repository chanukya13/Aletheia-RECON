## 1. Trace an ingredient interpretation back to the person or text that introduced it

A researcher selects *cropleac* and asks where the proposed identities enter the record. The selected Old English excerpt in Cockayne’s printed edition contains *cropleac*. His translation gives *cropleek*. His editorial footnote tentatively proposes *Allium oleraceum?*. A later paper explicitly discusses onion and leek as possible readings. Each is a different kind of evidence for a different claim. [Historical edition and reuse terms](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/dq5gv7ch) · [Modern reconstruction and ambiguity discussion](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7387442/)

**New behavior.** Search a term across the pinned local sources, inspect the exact wording, and exclude one source contribution to see which interpretations still have a recorded basis. The reader and scenario editor already support inspecting individual spans and choosing identities. This feature starts a step earlier by explaining why each candidate appears at all.

- Build a read-only projection from source revisions, spans, assertions and candidate support IDs. Attach a versioned role to individual spans, since a translation and an editor’s note can share one page. Record separately whether a passage contains the literal word, proposes an identification or describes a modern material used in a study. Record the edition/work relationship so two pages from Cockayne are displayed together.
- Provide two distinct result lists. Literal matches retain original offsets and exact text. Linked interpretations require an explicit assertion path to a span. A spelling variant can enter the second list only through a declared mapping. Unclassified matches display their available wording and source.
- Implement exclusion as a query over the supporting paths. Removing one citation leaves other valid paths visible. Keep the source revision and all saved assertions intact. Scope an empty result to the indexed passages. Label interpretation links as draft and allow a click through to the existing source reader or scenario.
- Start with the eyesalve source chain. Use the oxymel passage as a boundary test. A passage about use must not supply missing ingredient quantities or become a complete recipe.
- **Acceptance.** Replay the same query over pinned sources, verify offsets, and compare its results with literal-only search. Excluding the editorial note removes its specific tentative support, while the original word and the modern paper’s alternatives remain inspectable. Browser-test exclusion, restoration and exact-source navigation at desktop and phone widths. Report retrieval behavior on this declared example. Independent judgments would be required for semantic precision or recall.

## 2. Compare the measured outcomes of alternative reconstructions

A researcher choosing between onion and leek can ask whether those two modern preparations gave the same measured result in a named assay. Fuchs et al. report composition in Table 1 and median minimum inhibitory concentration (MIC) in Table 2. For *S. aureus* ATCC 6538, the onion and leek formulations both read 0.625% v/v. For LAC they read 2.5% and 1.25% v/v, respectively. These are study-level descriptive contrasts tied to the reported formulations and endpoints. [Published Tables 1 and 2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6261618/#pone.0208108.t002)

**New behavior.** The evidence workspace already displays individual outcomes. Add a matched contrast between two measured arms. Return the underlying cells and the reasons a comparison is eligible or unavailable. Link to it from the historical interpretation, with each arm labeled as a modern reconstruction. Preserve the distinction between the historical reading and the laboratory material.

- Read Tables 1 and 2 from the locally pinned JATS XML with the existing XML parser. Key each observation by source revision, table, organism/strain, formulation column, statistic and unit. Capture the cell coordinates, original text, qualifier and matched material version where available. Fail explicitly on an unknown header or changed source hash.
- Start with the two existing *S. aureus* strains and the two existing material versions. Check the same study, strain, assay protocol, endpoint, statistic and units before calculating the onion/leek ratio of reported medians. Show both inputs and calculation direction. A duplicate key, missing context, censored value or incompatible endpoint yields the exact reason a ratio is unavailable. List the reported preparation differences, including the authors’ use of leek greens. Interpretation of this descriptive ratio does not extend to ingredient causation or clinical effect.
- Use the published table as a reference for data extraction. Freeze expected row/column identities independently of the parser. Check all 16 numeric cells of Table 2, including those outside the initial two-strain UI. Demonstrate that shuffled columns still resolve correctly, that a missing cell stays missing, and that the 2020 biofilm endpoint cannot be divided by a 2018 broth MIC. A simple paper-level lookup can be checked against these cell identities. Keep cell fidelity separate from authentic historical-identity accuracy.
- **Acceptance.** In a real browser, switch strains and obtain 1.0 and 2.0 respectively for onion divided by leek. Open each cited cell, reproduce the calculation offline from the pinned file, and verify an incompatible comparison produces a reason. Report achieved checks only after execution.

## Delivery order and scope

Build the source trace first while Track 1 is the priority. Use the existing contracts, TypeScript core, Fastify routes, React workspaces and source dialogs. Both features can operate on the archived inputs without a new dependency, external service or independent reviewer. Keep the feature inputs read-only and the original corpus draft. Treat optional inclusion in portable exports as a versioned extension after the two read-only workflows pass.

The event lists 11:00 EET on 8 November, or 14:30 IST. For an immediate start around midday IST, budget roughly 50 minutes for one functioning term trace, 40 minutes for the two-strain contrast, and at least 25 minutes for integration and real-browser checks. These are time boxes, not measured implementation times. If the first workflow overruns its box, finish and verify it before starting the second. [Official schedule and criteria](https://ethnohack2026.devpost.com/)

## Basis for the choice

Track 1 calls for mining historical material, mapping plant names across eras, identifying uses and connecting them to modern pharmacology. Judge Antigoni Cheilari publicly describes EthnoBOT, EthnoDB and Phytocode, including a corpus of roughly 350 Balkan studies. Petros Bouras-Vallianatos publishes editions, translations and research on medical lexicography. Nektarios Aligiannis studies plant extraction and biological evaluation. Dimitrios Gunopulos works on data mining, and Mehmet Can Ay has published on harmonizing biomedical data across studies. The proposed product choices are our inference from that work and the official criteria.
